Unusual local results (statistical screen, not evidence of fraud)

Method: every municipality's official 2026 TSE count is compared with the same municipality's 2022 runoff result (TSE open data). Flags: turnout ≥95% of registered voters, or up ≥15 pts vs 2022; Flávio's share swing vs Bolsonaro 2022 that deviates ≥3 standard deviations (and ≥8 pts) or ≥25 pts from the state's median swing (≥2,000 valid votes); either candidate ≥97% of valid votes (≥1,000 votes); blank+null share up ≥10 pts. Partial counts can be unrepresentative, and small or remote municipalities have historically shown extreme margins and turnout swings naturally (local politics, a few large families, polling-site changes). A flag means "worth a look", nothing more.
